WHAT LANDED
`lib/seo/ai-crawlers.ts` is the registry: nine user-agent tokens, each carrying
its class (search | training), owner and purpose. `lib/seo/robots-txt.ts` renders
the store's robots.txt from it. `app/store/[slug]/robots.txt/route.ts` now reads
the plan and the policy off the row `getCurrentTenant` already resolved — no
extra query — and a Pro-only `PUT /api/tenant-admin/seo/ai-crawlers` writes the
policy into `tenants.settings`. New "AI Crawlers" tab in the SEO Manager
(Pro-only), plus an `ai-crawlers` entry in `SEO_PRO_FEATURES` so a Basic tenant
meets the locked card.

THE ROBOTS.TXT RULE THAT SHAPED THE DESIGN. A crawler that matches its own
`User-agent` group uses THAT group and ignores the `*` group entirely (RFC 9309
§2.2.1). So a blocked bot gets its own group with `Disallow: /` (complete on its
own — the admin paths need no repeating), and an ALLOWED bot gets no group at
all. Publishing `User-agent: GPTBot` / `Allow: /` to "be explicit" would have
taken GPTBot out of the wildcard group and handed it `/api/`, `/tenant-admin/`
and `/auth/`. Hence 'open' renders comments and nothing functional — asserted
(`userAgents(txt)` is exactly `["*"]` under 'open').

TWO PARSERS, TWO DIRECTIONS, ON PURPOSE. The PLAN fails closed ('basic' — a DB
blip must not hand out Pro); the POLICY fails open ('open' — an unreadable
settings blob must never be read as "this store asked to be hidden from AI
answers"). Both directions are table-tested.

BYTE-IDENTICAL FOR BASIC. The pre-story file is frozen as a literal in the test
(not derived from the renderer, which would make the assertion tautological) and
asserted for plan 'basic' AND for every unreadable plan value, including a tenant
that stored 'blocked' while on Pro and then downgraded — the setting stays in the
column, dormant. The storefront never blocks on plan.

NO xAI ROW SHIPS — an evidenced deviation from the story's AC, stated here
rather than papered over. The AC asks for "xAI search UA". xAI publishes no
crawler user-agent token: x.ai's own robots.txt names other operators' bots and
not its own (curled 2026-08-14), and Cloudflare's AI Crawl Control bot reference
lists no xAI crawler. Third-party directories disagree with each other between
`GrokBot`, `xAI-Grok` and `xAI-Crawler`. Emitting a guessed token would tell an
owner they had blocked something they had not — the one failure this feature
cannot have. The registry is data; the row is one line the day xAI documents it.
Tokens that DID ship were verified against primary sources on 2026-08-14:
developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots, privacy.claude.com article 8896518,
docs.perplexity.ai/guides/bots, cross-checked against Cloudflare's bot reference.

COPY IS THE FEATURE, AND IT IS TESTED. Owners arrive believing "AI bots" is one
thing. Each class states what refusing it COSTS: blocking search = "absent from
AI answers"; blocking training = "does not remove you from AI answers today".
The policy note says plainly that robots.txt is "a published request, not a
lock" and that a crawler ignoring it is not stopped. User-triggered fetchers
(ChatGPT-User, Claude-User, Perplexity-User) are deliberately ABSENT from the
registry with a note saying why — their operators publish that robots.txt does
not apply to a request a person made directly, so listing them under a policy
switch would offer control this file does not have. No option summary may promise
a citation — asserted by regex.

SETTINGS KEY BOUNDED BY LENGTH, NOT BY ENUM — the `reorderReminderDays` /
verification-token split, for the documented reason: `parseTenantSettings` fails
as a UNIT, so an out-of-enum value hand-edited into the column would return `{}`
and take the tenant's tagline, colours and cookie banner down with it. The enum
is enforced by the write route on the way in (`z.enum` + `.strict()`) and
re-applied by `parseAiCrawlerPolicy` on the way out. Asserted.

GATES. `requirePermission("canEditSeo")` wrapping `requireFeature(SEO_PRO)`, the
sanctioned order — a member without the permission is refused BEFORE the plan
read (asserted: `tenants.findFirst` never called). Basic → 403 `upgrade_required`
and no write. The settings merge is read-modify-write over the RAW blob, so keys
this platform version has never heard of survive (asserted). Audit row
`seo.ai_crawlers_updated` records the transition `{from, to}`.
